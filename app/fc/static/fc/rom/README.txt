The character ROM of the monitor of a program window (fc/program.js).

The glyphs are those of GNU Unifont 18.0.01, Plane 0, from
https://unifoundry.com/pub/unifont/unifont-18.0.01/font-builds/unifont-18.0.01.hex.gz
tools/rom.py of fc wrote them in pages of 256 characters: <page>.bin, where
page is (code >> 8) in three hex digits. A page is 256 widths (0, 8 or 16),
then 256 glyphs of 16 rows of 16 bits.
